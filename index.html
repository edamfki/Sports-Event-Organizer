Event setup: name, dates, pools and format
Pool settings
Pools, then knockout: each pool is a round robin (everybody meets everybody once); the levels (lines) of the player list decide who is the strongest and every pool receives one player of each level; the days after the pool days are used for the knockout. Knockout only: no pools, the players are seeded in the order you enter them.
Players by level (one line per level, 4 names per line, strongest line first)
Paste or edit as text instead
Write each player's full name in his own field (first name and family name). You can paste a whole table from Excel into the first field: rows are levels, columns are players. Press Enter to jump to the next field. If only family names are added to existing players (same first names, same places), the draw, scores and schedule stay as they are.
Player availability: who cannot play on a day (used by the schedule)
Type the names as in the player lists (first name, family name or full name), separated by commas. Pool matches of these players are moved to days when they can play (a player who is unavailable on too many pool days cannot be scheduled within the daily maximum). A player never plays more than the daily maximum; the round table is kept as far as possible (all matches of a round on the same day, rounds in order, level 1 vs level 2 last). Knockout matches can only be checked, because they depend on the results.
